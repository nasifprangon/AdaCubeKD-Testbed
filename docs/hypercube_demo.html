Phased Hypercube Embedding

Duan et al.'s recursive matching builds the cube one dimension at a time. AdaCubeKD keeps the procedure and changes the edge weight per phase, so the same clients form a different cube early, mid, and late in training.

Synthetic data. Clients get a Dirichlet(α) label split over 10 classes. Each client's average reference-set prediction after warm-up is modeled as its label histogram mixed 65/35 with uniform. Physical positions are random in a unit square, linked when closer than 0.36 (plus bridging links if the graph is disconnected). Matching follows Duan et al.: virtual nodes are matched by maximum summed weight. Member alignment tries every bit-flip alignment (2 at dimension 2, 4 at dimension 3) instead of pairing randomly; flips keep each dimension's edges together, which the round-robin schedule relies on. In training, dij is re-measured at each phase boundary; this demo uses one snapshot for all phases.
